METHODOLOGY & DATA

OTM turns market signals into a commercial action queue.

v0 deliberately separates observed facts, modeled prioritization and missing client inputs.

1ObserveMarket, channel, city, chain and menu signals
2ModelScale × revenue weight × Anchor fit
3PrioritizeRelative index, tiers and recommended applications

FORMULA

Relative opportunity index

V0
raw score = observed outlets × relative revenue weight × Anchor fitindex = raw score ÷ highest score in comparison set × 100

For brands, the comparison set is the 15-brand sample. For categories and cities, it is the visible market set. The result ranks relative priority; it is not currency.

No PHP value is shown because average revenue, dairy incidence, Anchor share and conversion assumptions have not been calibrated.

SOURCE & LIMITATIONS

Audit trail

  • Observed: market, channel, city, brand sample, ingredient, flavor and process shares from the included 2026Q2 source workbook.
  • Modeled: relative revenue weight, Anchor fit, normalized index and sample percentile tier.
  • Not available: Anchor sell-in/sell-out, distributor coverage, SKU mix, outlet master, account owner, conversion stage and exact addresses.
  • Use: workshop prioritization and data-contract alignment. Do not use v0 as a sales forecast or definitive coverage list.
